How I work

A practical analysis lifecycle designed to reduce ambiguity before it reaches delivery.

My role is not only to document what stakeholders say. I structure the problem, make rules and dependencies explicit, and create enough clarity for teams to build and test confidently.

STEP 01

Start with the business outcome.

Clarify the problem to solve, expected outcome, scope boundaries, stakeholders and constraints before discussing solution detail.

Process + Decision Thinking

Keep workflow and decision logic clear, separate and traceable.

BPMN answers how work moves. Decision modelling clarifies how a business outcome is determined. Keeping both explicit makes complex workflows easier to review, test and change.
